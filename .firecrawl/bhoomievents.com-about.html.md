WHO WE ARE

## Our history

Weddings are magical milestones, woven with dreams, adorned with flowers, and celebrated with grandeur. At **Bhoomi Events & Planners**, we understand that your wedding day is one of the most cherished moments of your life. That’s why we offer **professional wedding planning services** that turn your vision into a breathtaking reality. From traditional ceremonies to modern celebrations, our expert team ensures every detail is handled with precision, creativity, and a touch of elegance—making your big day truly unforgettable.

### Established Experts in Wedding Planning and Event Management Since 2007

Founded in 2007 by visionary entrepreneurs **Mr. Dinesh Pandya** and **Mrs. Nikita Pandya, Bhoomi Events** brings over 18 years of hands-on experience in the **event planning industry**. We specialize not only in weddings but also in managing corporate conferences, formal parties, musical concerts, conventions, and more. Whether it's a grand celebration or an intimate gathering, we approach every event with professionalism, passion, and personalized care. Our commitment to **creating extraordinary experiences** ensures that every event reflects your values, style, and story—delivered with integrity and excellence.


## Meet the Visionary   Behind Bhoomi Events

### Mr. Dinesh Pandya

**Mr. Dinesh Pandya**, Founder & Chairperson of **Bhoomi Events & Planners**, is a dynamic first-generation entrepreneur with over two decades of hands-on experience in the **event management industry**. His deep passion for travel has given him a unique perspective on global cultures, customs, and traditions—allowing him to blend **traditional Indian elegance** with **modern international trends** in every event he curates.

At **Bhoomi Events**, Mr. Pandya's commitment to excellence and eye for detail shape the brand’s identity. His **larger-than-life approach** and relentless dedication to quality help turn each event into an unforgettable celebration. From luxury weddings to grand corporate gatherings, he ensures every project reflects creativity, class, and precision.

For Mr. Dinesh, Bhoomi Events isn’t just a business—it’s a labor of love, a handpicked curation of the best people and skills in the industry. Under his leadership, the company has grown into a trusted name known for managing complex events with grace, turning every challenge into an opportunity for brilliance.
