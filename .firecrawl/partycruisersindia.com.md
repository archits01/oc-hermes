## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Party Cruisers Limited — from planning luxury destination weddings to executing large-scale events across India and the Middle East.


What does Party Cruisers Limited do?


Party Cruisers Limited (PCL) is India's premier luxury event management company, operating since 1994. We specialise in bespoke weddings, luxury destination weddings, corporate events, social celebrations, artist management, and venue booking across India and the Middle East — through six dedicated specialist brand divisions.


Is Party Cruisers Limited a listed company?


Yes. Party Cruisers Limited has been listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India since 2021, making it India's first and only publicly listed event management company. This brings a level of transparency, governance, and accountability that is unique in the industry.


What are the 6 brand divisions of Party Cruisers Limited?


PCL operates through six specialist brands: House of Vivaah (wedding design, décor & production), Vows Vachan (end-to-end wedding planning & curation), Event Factory (corporate event management), Party House (social & milestone celebrations), Live Space (entertainment & artist management), and Venue Affairs (venue sourcing & management).


How much does a luxury wedding planner in Mumbai cost?


Luxury wedding planning in Mumbai varies based on scale, services, and the number of events involved. A full-service luxury package at Party Cruisers Limited — covering décor, planning, entertainment, and venue management — is tailored to each client's vision and guest count. We recommend reaching out for a personalised consultation and quote.


Does Party Cruisers Limited plan destination weddings?


Absolutely. Destination weddings are one of our specialties, both domestically — in Goa, Rajasthan, Hyderabad, and beyond — and internationally across the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman. Our Vows Vachan and Venue Affairs divisions collaborate to make destination weddings seamless from logistics to last-minute details.


In which cities does Party Cruisers Limited operate?


PCL is headquartered in Mumbai with offices in Pune, Nashik, Goa, Hyderabad, Lucknow, and Delhi across India. In the Middle East, we operate from our Dubai headquarters with presence in Doha, Bahrain, and Oman.


Can Party Cruisers Limited manage both weddings and corporate events?


Yes. Unlike most event companies that focus on one segment, PCL's six-division structure allows us to bring specialist expertise to both personal celebrations and professional events. Vows Vachan and House of Vivaah handle weddings while Event Factory specialises exclusively in corporate impression management.
