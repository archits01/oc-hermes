## Frequently asked questions

What services do event management companies in Mumbai offer?

Top event management companies in Mumbai like Theevexa offer end-to-end services including venue booking, décor & theme design, catering, entertainment, photography & videography, guest hospitality, logistics, invitation design and on-ground production for weddings, corporate events, brand activations, conferences and private parties across Mumbai — from BKC and Bandra to South Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.

How much do event management companies in Mumbai charge?

Event management charges in Mumbai typically start from ₹3–6 lakh for intimate gatherings and go up to ₹60 lakh+ for large luxury weddings and corporate events. Most planners charge a flat planning fee (8–15% of total budget) or a customised package. Theevexa offers transparent, itemised quotes with no hidden costs.

Which is the best event management company in Mumbai?

Theevexa is consistently rated among the best event management companies in Mumbai, with 10+ years of experience, 1200+ successful events, a 4.9-star rating and tie-ups with all top Mumbai venues including the Taj, Oberoi, JW Marriott and Sofitel BKC.

How to hire an event management company in Mumbai?

To hire an event management company in Mumbai: (1) shortlist 2–3 planners based on portfolio and reviews, (2) book a free discovery call to share your event date, guest count and budget, (3) review the proposal and moodboards, (4) sign a clear scope-of-work contract with a 25–30% advance. With Theevexa you can book a free consultation by filling our contact form.

Do event management companies in Mumbai handle corporate events?

Yes. Leading event management companies in Mumbai like Theevexa specialise in corporate events including annual conferences, product launches, dealer meets, award nights, team off-sites, AGMs and brand activations across BKC, Lower Parel, Powai, Andheri and Navi Mumbai.
